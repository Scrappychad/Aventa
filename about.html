About

The person behind the pass.

Who's behind the lens

Hi, I'm Nana.

NanaGraphy started as a way to make photography feel personal again: less about a stiff, formal shoot, and more about capturing a real moment in someone's life, exactly as it is.

Over time, the sessions that meant the most weren't the ones booked for an occasion. They were the ones someone gave to another person, as a way of saying "I see you, and I wanted you to have this."

The idea behind Aventa

Why a photoshoot became a pass.

Aventa exists because the best gifts aren't things. They're experiences, held for someone until they're ready for them. So instead of booking a slot on a calendar, Aventa works like a pass: purchased once, attached to a person, and redeemed whenever the moment is right.

Whether you're keeping one for yourself or sending one to a friend, a partner, or someone who just needs a good day, Aventa is built to make photography feel like a gift, not an appointment.
