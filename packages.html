FAQs

Can I gift an Aventa?+

Yes. Choose any package, select "Gift an Aventa" at booking, and the recipient will receive their Aventa Pass and redeem it whenever they're ready.

Can I buy one for myself?+

Absolutely. Choose "Book for myself" and pick your preferred date.

Does it expire?+

It remains valid until redeemed, according to NanaGraphy's redemption policy.

Can I reschedule?+

Yes, subject to availability.

Can I upgrade my package?+

Yes, you can upgrade by paying the difference between packages.

What should I wear?+

Style guidance will be provided before your session.

Can I choose my location?+

Yes, subject to discussion and suitability.
